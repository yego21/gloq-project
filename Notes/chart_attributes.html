Astrology JSON Attribute Reference

houses[]

AttributeDescription
house.signZodiac sign on the cusp
house.degreeDegree of cusp within sign
house.numberHouse number (1–12)
house.cusp_longitudeAbsolute ecliptic longitude

planets[]

AttributeDescription
planet.namePlanet name (e.g. Sun, Moon)
planet.signZodiac sign
planet.houseHouse number
planet.degreeDegree within sign
planet.symbolUnicode glyph
planet.elementElement classification (Earth, Air, Fire, Water)
planet.longitudeAbsolute ecliptic longitude

aspects[]

AttributeDescription
aspect.orbOrb difference in degrees
aspect.angleExact angular separation
aspect.planet1First planet involved
aspect.planet2Second planet involved
aspect.aspect_typeType of aspect (Conjunction, Trine, etc.)
